## Try it in a minute
1. Open Claude on the web, in the Claude Desktop app or in the mobile app.
2. In the message box, select **Cowork**. If you do not see **Chat** and **Cowork** options, you have the new Claude experience and can skip this step.
3. Describe the outcome you want, the format, and the files, links or apps Claude should work from.
4. Review Claude's approach, then let it run.
5. Come back from any surface to pick up the finished output.

## Example
```text
In the folder I have connected there are six weeks of exported on-call reports as CSV files.

Produce: (1) an Excel workbook with one tab per week and a summary tab with formulas for pages per service and mean time to acknowledge; (2) a two-page Word summary of the trends and the five alerts most worth fixing. Do not modify or delete the original files.
```

## At work
- **Reports from messy inputs.** Cowork generates Excel files with working formulas and multiple tabs, and formatted documents, from a folder of raw exports.
- **Work that continues.** In cloud sessions Claude keeps working when you close your laptop or step away.
- **Standing preferences.** Global instructions set tone, output format and background on your role for every session.

## Pitfalls
- Cowork is for paid plans. Local file access, browser use and computer use need the Claude Desktop app open and connected.
- In **Skip all approvals** nothing reviews Claude's actions. For work with real consequences, such as messages sent as you, stay close and review what Claude does.
- Avoid granting access to local files with sensitive information.
- Multi-step tasks use more of your usage than a quick question. Group related work into one task.
- Sessions cannot be shared with others, though artifacts created in a session can.
