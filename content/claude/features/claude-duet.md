## Try it in a minute
1. Go to the [Claude Duet product page](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet) and select **Join the waitlist**.
2. Enter your name and the email address on your Claude account, your plan and your phone type, then select **Submit**.
3. When the invitation email arrives, open it on your phone and follow the setup steps for iPhone or Android. Your memory, skills and connectors are already there.
4. Send a first request, typed or as a voice message, and watch the **Tasks** tab for anything that needs your input.

## Example
```text
Pull together a brief on this company before my 2 pm meeting.

Use my email and calendar to find who I am meeting and what we last discussed. Add recent public news about them. Keep it to one page and tell me which email or file each fact came from.
```

## At work
- **Morning brief.** Each morning it looks across your email and calendar, picks what most needs attention, and can draft replies for you to review.
- **Larger jobs.** Bigger results, such as a research brief or a slide deck, appear in the **Make** tab.
- **Sites without a connector.** It uses its own browser in the cloud.

## Pitfalls
- It is a research preview, available through a waitlist to Pro, Max and Ultra plans in the US. It is not available on Team or Enterprise plans yet.
- You cannot choose a model, watch it work in its browser or take over the browser.
- It checks your accounts at set intervals, so something urgent can arrive between checks.
- It cannot see the files on your computer or all of your projects, and Microsoft 365 is not available yet.
- Content it reads can contain instructions meant to mislead it, so review drafts and approval requests.
