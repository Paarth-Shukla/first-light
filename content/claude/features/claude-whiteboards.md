## Try it in a minute
1. Ask Claude for a whiteboard in any chat, or go to the **Artifacts** tab and select the tile labeled **Whiteboard**.
2. Describe what you want to work through, or select the microphone next to the send button and dictate a brain dump.
3. Ask Claude to regroup the board by theme or turn it into a timeline.
4. Drag across a region of the board to ask Claude about just that part.
5. When the thinking is done, ask Claude to turn the board into a doc or deck.

## Example
```text
Map this process on a whiteboard from start to finish: what happens when a customer array reports a failed drive.

Steps I know: alert fires, support case opens, on-call triages, replacement ships, field tech swaps the drive, rebuild completes. Put the steps in order, group them by owning team, and tell me what is missing between "replacement ships" and "rebuild completes".
```

## At work
- **Retros and planning.** Everyone on the board works on it live and can rearrange what is there, and you can follow a teammate around the board.
- **Finding gaps in a process.** Ask what is missing between two steps. Claude adds its suggestions to the board for you to check.
- **From sketch to document.** A prompt such as "Turn this board into a project plan" converts the settled structure into a doc.

## Pitfalls
- Whiteboards is in beta on paid plans, and off by default on Enterprise plans until an owner turns it on.
- Claude works from what is on the board and does not use context from your connected apps.
- You can add pictures and videos, but you cannot import a board from a file. File export is PNG.
- It is not available to organizations with HIPAA-ready configurations.
