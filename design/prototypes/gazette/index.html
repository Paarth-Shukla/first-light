Claude Desk one technique to try at work today

Quotes first, then the answer

Reach for it when you ask a question of a long document, such as a contract, an incident report or a design spec, and need an answer you can check.

Put the document at the top of the prompt. Have Claude copy out the passages that bear on the question before it answers, and cite them by number. The quotes show you at a glance whether the answer stands on the text.

Read the quotes before the answer. A claim with no quote behind it is a claim to check yourself.

The prompt
<document>
{{paste the document here}}
</document>

First find the passages in the document that bear on my question. Copy them word for word inside <quotes> tags, numbered.

Then answer inside <answer> tags, citing the quote numbers you relied on. If the quotes do not settle the question, say so plainly instead of guessing.
